AI-Powered Learning Roadmap & Study Planner ✅ (chosen) – Generates customized, step-by-step study schedules with curated resources and daily micro-quizzes based on a user's target skill and available time.

Problem Statement

Self-directed learners and bootcamp students often feel overwhelmed by the abundance of online resources and struggle to structure a realistic, consistent learning path. Without clear milestones and active recall, learners frequently abandon their goals. An AI-driven planner solves this by breaking complex topics into manageable daily tasks and reinforcing knowledge through interactive checkpoints.

MVP Features

Goal & Availability Input: Users enter their target skill/topic, current proficiency level, and hours available per week.

Dynamic Roadmap Generation: AI generates a structured, week-by-week syllabus with clear milestones and recommended topics.

Daily Micro-Quiz Generator: AI creates a 3-to-5 question quiz tailored to the current day's topic to verify comprehension.

Progress Tracker: Interactive checklist allowing users to mark modules as complete and dynamically adjust remaining timelines.
